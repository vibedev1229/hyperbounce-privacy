Privacy Policy for Hyper Bounce (iOS)

Effective Date: June 29, 2026

Last Updated: September 20, 2026

This is the policy for the iOS (App Store) version of Hyper Bounce. The Android version uses different services for purchases, leaderboards and advertising consent, and has its own policy here.

1. Introduction

Hyper Bounce is developed by VibeDeV and published by VIBECORE DIGITAL ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our iOS game application.

We do not operate our own servers, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not build personal profiles. The game is fully playable offline.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Information

  • Google AdMob (advertising): AdMob may collect an advertising identifier, device information, app-interaction data, and approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address in order to serve and measure ads. AdMob does not receive precise/GPS location from this app.
  • Game Progress: High scores, unlocked levels, your chosen name, audio and screen settings, and used-redeem-code markers are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to us.
  • Crash and Diagnostic Data: Anonymous crash and performance data, and only where your device is set to share it with developers. You control this in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.
  • Purchases: The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We receive confirmation that a purchase occurred so we can remove ads; we never see or store your payment details.
  • Game Center (optional): If you sign in to Game Center, your Game Center nickname and your scores are shared with Apple and shown on a public leaderboard, and other players' nicknames and scores are shown to you. Sign-in is optional; a local best-scores list works without it. Your Game Center identity is managed by your Apple Account.

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not ask you for personal identification information (your real name, email address, or phone number). The only name involved is your Game Center nickname, which is handled by Apple as described above.
  • Precise or GPS location data
  • Camera, photo library, or microphone access
  • Contact list or personal files

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve the game experience
  • Display advertisements through AdMob
  • Diagnose crashes and fix technical issues
  • Save your game progress and settings locally on your device
  • Show optional leaderboards via Game Center

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share limited technical data with Google AdMob (subject to Google's Privacy Policy) and, where you choose to sign in, with Apple's Game Center (subject to Apple's Privacy Policy). Redeem codes are validated entirely on your device, are single-use per device, and are never linked to any personal information.

5. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On first launch the app presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, asking permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. This permission is used only to allow personalised advertising.

Declining is fully supported. If you decline, the game works exactly the same and you simply see non-personalised ads instead. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. Advertising Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where required, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we gather your advertising consent using Google's User Messaging Platform before any ads are initialised. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time via Privacy settings in the game's Settings screen. Withholding consent results in non-personalised ads; the game remains fully playable.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app is family-friendly but is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately.

8. Data Security

Game progress is stored locally on your device. We implement appropriate measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction of data. Because we operate no server, there is no account of yours for us to lose.

9. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google AdMob together with Google's User Messaging Platform, and Apple's Game Center and App Store services. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

10. Your Choices

  • Tracking: Decline Apple's tracking prompt, or change it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining does not limit gameplay.
  • Ad personalisation: Where the EEA/UK/Swiss consent prompt applies, change or withdraw consent via Privacy settings in the game's Settings screen.
  • Remove Ads: A one-time purchase removes banner and interstitial ads. The entitlement is tied to your Apple Account and restores on reinstall.
  • Data deletion: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data. We hold no server-side data about you. Your Game Center profile is managed by Apple and can be managed at appleid.apple.com.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the Last Updated date at the top of this policy.
