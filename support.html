Hyper Bounce Support

Hyper Bounce is an original neon arcade brick-breaker by VibeDeV, published by VIBECORE DIGITAL.

Need help, found a bug, or have a question?
Email support@vibecoredigital.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

  • I bought Remove Ads and reinstalled. How do I get it back?
    Remove Ads is a one-time purchase tied to your store account and restores automatically when you reopen the game. If it does not, email us with the store account's region and the approximate purchase date.
  • My progress disappeared.
    Progress, high scores and settings are stored only on your device, so they are removed if you uninstall the game or reset the device. They are not held on any server of ours.
  • The leaderboard looks empty.
    The global leaderboard fills in as other players post scores, so it can look sparse at first. You also need to be signed in to see it: Google Play Games on Android, Game Center on iOS. Your own best runs on that device always show, signed in or not.
  • How do I change my ad/consent choice?
    Where the EEA/UK consent prompt applies, open Settings → Privacy settings inside the game. On iOS, Hyper Bounce does not ask to track you and its ads are non-personalised, so there is no tracking permission to change.
  • Do I need an account?
    No. There is no account, login or registration. The game is fully playable offline the moment it launches.
